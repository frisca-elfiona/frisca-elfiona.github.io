Big Mac index: my version

Does adjusting for income change how expensive a Big Mac is in each country?

Open the chart in a new tab

What I found

Adjusting for income barely moves the typical Big Mac price gap with America: about 27% raw and 25% adjusted (July 2026, 51 countries). Indonesia is the cheapest at −62% and Switzerland the dearest at +45%.

Data credit

Source: Big Mac index data from The Economist's big-mac-data repository (data under CC BY 4.0; the repository's software under the MIT licence). Raw: the local price of a Big Mac converted at the market exchange rate and compared with the base country's price. GDP-adjusted: the same comparison after allowing for GDP per person.

This is my own recreation of the MSIN0167 course target, built as a class exercise with AI help. It is not an Economist publication.
